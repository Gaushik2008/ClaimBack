From rejection letter to appeal

The insurer must give a reason and point to your policy. ClaimBack tests that reason, step by step.

  • Read the rejection

    Pulls out the stated ground: waiting period, pre-existing disease, room rent, or “not medically necessary”.

  • Find the clause

    Matches the ground to the policy wording and confirms the clause really exists in your document.

  • Check the dates

    Works out waiting periods and continuity with plain date arithmetic, not a model’s guess.

  • Draft the appeal

    Writes to the insurer’s grievance officer first, then the Insurance Ombudsman, with the documents to attach.
