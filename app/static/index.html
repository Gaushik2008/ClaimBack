        Honest limits

        What ClaimBack won't do.

        It won't promise an outcome

        It gives advisory information. A strong-looking case can still lose, and the Ombudsman or a court decides.

        It can say "not enough information"

        When your documents don't settle the question, it says so instead of guessing.

        Its precedents have a bias

        Decided complaints come from a few Ombudsman centres, and some centres mostly side with insurers or with policyholders. A warning appears when that could skew what you see.

        It isn't a lawyer

        For a large claim or a complicated dispute, speak to a qualified professional. Accuracy figures will be published only after they are measured.
